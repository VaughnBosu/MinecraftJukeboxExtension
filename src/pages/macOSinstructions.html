Use Local Minecraft Audio on macOS

Unlock all discs in the Minecraft Jukebox extension by pointing it to the audio files already on your Mac. Follow these quick steps and you’ll have every track available.

Your files never leave your device. Chrome’s dialog says “Upload”, but the audio is only read from your local Minecraft install and saved inside the extension for playback in the popup.

Step-by-step (macOS)

  1. Open the extension popup and click Use Local Minecraft Audio.
  2. When the folder picker opens, press Command ⌘ + Shift + G to open “Go to the folder…”.
  3. Paste ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft and press Enter.
  4. Click the assets folder once (don’t open it), then choose Upload.
  5. Chrome asks “Upload N files to this site?” — click Upload to confirm.
  6. Wait a moment while the extension imports and saves your discs. Once complete, the grayed-out discs become playable; you can play, pause, skip, cue, and control volume as usual.

Troubleshooting

  • If the folder doesn’t appear, press Command ⌘ + Shift + . to show hidden folders.
  • Make sure you select the assets folder itself, not files inside it.
  • The upload confirmation can take a few seconds to appear — the assets folder holds thousands of files.
  • If nothing loads, double-check that Minecraft Java Edition is installed on this Mac.
